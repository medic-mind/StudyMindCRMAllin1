# ADR 0031 — Weekly webinar (live classes) auto-enrollment

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-06-08

## Context

StudyMind runs weekly live online classes — Biology, Chemistry, Physics and
Maths at GCSE and A-Level — that families pay for with Stripe subscriptions.
Today this is run by hand: spotting who has paid (the subject sometimes only
appears in the product name or even the customer name), keeping a class roster,
emailing the Zoom link and a schedule every week, remembering to rotate the Zoom
link, and stopping the emails when a subscription lapses. Ops asked the CRM to
own the whole loop with a simple UI, and to "find these people automatically".

## Decision

1. **A dedicated webinar domain**, not a reuse of Family/Booking. New tables:
   `WebinarCohort` (academic year), `WebinarHoliday`, `WebinarClass`
   (subject+level, weekly slot, Zoom link + rotation, send offset, syllabus),
   `WebinarSyllabusWeek`, `WebinarEnrollment`, `WebinarEmailDispatch` (idempotency
   log), `WebinarSettings` (singleton). The reconciliation triangle and the
   booking mirror are about hours and money; weekly classes are a distinct,
   simpler product and deserve their own shape.

2. **Deterministic matcher first, AI advisory only** (consistent with ADR 0030
   and golden rule #3). `detectWebinarClasses` reads Stripe-derived text and is
   authoritative. The `webinar_class_match` mini-task is consulted *only* when
   the rules find nothing, and its suggestions always land as `pending_review`.
   To reconcile the request for "automatic" enrolment with "AI suggests, humans
   confirm": a confident **rule** match (explicit subject + level, confidence
   ≥ 0.8) auto-activates; everything else — every AI suggestion, every
   ambiguous match — waits in a one-click review queue. Every enrolment is
   audited.

3. **Email via the existing Google OAuth mailbox**, not Resend. The team already
   connects Gmail, and `sendSystemEmail` (§14) supports attachments. Weekly
   emails carry the Zoom link and a PDF schedule generated by the dependency-free
   PDF writer (ADR 0021); an uploaded syllabus PDF overrides the generated one.
   This honours the request to "use the Google OAuth that is already set up".

4. **Idempotent, holiday-aware scheduling.** Sessions are computed from the
   cohort dates minus holidays; wall-clock slots convert to UTC DST-correctly.
   The dispatcher claims a unique `(enrollmentId, weekNumber)` row before
   sending, so retries and overlapping runs never double-send. Only `active`
   enrolments are emailed, so a lapsed subscription stops the links with no
   extra branch.

5. **Jobs at the app boundary.** `packages/jobs` cannot import integrations, so
   the four recurring functions live in
   `apps/web/app/api/inngest/_boundary/webinar.ts` (dispatch hourly, expire
   hourly, detect daily, Zoom-rotation reminder weekly). Pure logic lives in
   `packages/core/src/webinar/` and `apps/web/lib/webinar/`.

## Consequences

- Live Stripe (`STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`) and a connected Google mailbox are required
  for detection and sending in production; both already exist in the stack.
- Subjects are stored as strings (not a DB enum) so admins can add subjects
  without a migration; the matcher's keyword set is the place to teach new ones.
- Future work: per-student (vs per-payer) enrolment, a booking-site push, and a
  "preview this week's email" action are natural extensions; the dispatch log
  already gives us the audit trail to build send-history reporting.
